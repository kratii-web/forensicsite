Career Guidance

Your Path to Forensic Excellence

A comprehensive roadmap to building your career in forensic science — from education to professional practice.

STEP 01

Foundation — Bachelor's Degree

Begin with a B.Sc. in Forensic Science, Chemistry, Biology, or a related discipline. Build a strong foundation in natural sciences, analytical chemistry, and basic criminalistics. Focus on laboratory skills and scientific methodology.

STEP 02

Specialization — Master's Degree

Pursue an M.Sc. in Forensic Science with specialization in DNA Forensics, Toxicology, Cyber Forensics, Questioned Documents, or Forensic Psychology. This deepens expertise and opens advanced career paths.

STEP 03

Practical Training & Internships

Gain hands-on experience through internships at FSL (Forensic Science Laboratories), police departments, or private forensic firms. Work on real cases under expert supervision. Build your portfolio of analyzed evidence.

STEP 04

Certification & Competitive Exams

Clear NET/SET exams for academic positions, or prepare for state/central forensic lab recruitment examinations. Obtain professional certifications from bodies like IAI, ABC, or ABFT to enhance credibility.

STEP 05

Professional Career

Launch your career as a Forensic Scientist, Crime Scene Investigator, Toxicologist, DNA Analyst, Cyber Forensic Expert, or Forensic Consultant. Opportunities exist in government labs, law enforcement, judiciary, and private sector.
